Methodology and honest caveats

  • Routed is not remediated. DISA manual STIGs harden configuration; they do not patch individual CVEs. A KEV entry routed to "Windows" means your Windows estate's hardening conversation belongs to a baseline we maintain — not that a specific rule neutralizes that CVE.
  • The routing table is curated. KEV product/vendor text is matched against the platform families we maintain (Windows, macOS, RHEL 7/8/9, Ubuntu 20.04/22.04/24.04, vSphere 6.7, Firefox; Proxmox baseline in build). Versioned in the kevstig repo — corrections are welcome as pull requests.
  • The gap is the artifact. The uncovered count (appliances, web apps, network gear, closed ecosystems) is published deliberately: it is the honest boundary of our maintained set and the workload plan behind our crosswalk work.
  • Source: CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog (JSON feed), refreshed daily by an automated job; baseline counts measured from the maintained checklists at generation time.
