What the crosswalk claims (and what it never will)

  • Routed = a KEV entry's vendor/product text matches an ordered, curated family whose platform key matches a maintained baseline. Curation carries exclusion guards: lookalike products (Aria Operations for Networks, Horizon DaaS, IKE "key exchange" strings) stay honestly uncovered.
  • Routed is not remediated. DISA manual STIGs harden configuration; the parsed STIG XMLs carry zero CVE references (verified). The claim is accountability, not patching.
  • The gap is the artifact. The uncovered majority is published on purpose — it is the workload plan below.
